Protecting Patient Information

A plain-language look at how patient intake data is protected end to end, and what's been fixed so far.
These fixes are now live in the system and have been verified working - patient submissions were tested end to end after the change and completed successfully.

What's been fixed

🔑

We hold our own key now

Fixed

Patient data is locked with a key that we control, not one the cloud provider manages on our behalf. If we ever need to prove who could access the data, we can.

🧾

Nothing gets lost forever

Fixed

If a file is ever accidentally changed or deleted, we keep the earlier copy so it can always be recovered.

🚧

No unlocked doors

Fixed

Any request that isn't sent over a secure, encrypted connection is refused outright - there's no way to reach patient data over an unprotected line.

🏢

Moved into the private work area

Fixed

The system that processes each submission now works inside our own private, walled-off network instead of a shared public space.

🪪

Only the access that's needed

Fixed

The system can only do the two things it's actually supposed to do (save a form, save a file) - nothing more. If it were ever compromised, the damage it could do is capped.

Still being worked on

📟

Health monitoring for the intake system

In progress

We don't yet get an automatic alert if the intake system is struggling or failing under load. Planned next.

🧐

Activity log at the front door

In progress

We don't yet keep a detailed record of who/what hit the front door (the public entry point) or automatically slow down suspicious bursts of traffic. Planned next.
